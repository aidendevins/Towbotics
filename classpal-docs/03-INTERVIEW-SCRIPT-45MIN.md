# ClassPal — 45-Minute Teacher Interview Script

**Purpose:** Validate product assumptions and output value with AP/IB high school teachers. Stay within 45 minutes. Prevent 2-hour rambling with strict sections and stop conditions.

**Rules:** One question per topic unless follow-up is explicitly listed. Use forced tradeoffs where indicated. In Output Validation (Section 4), collect ratings for every artifact; do not skip. Move on when the section time is up.

---

## INTERVIEW TIMING OVERVIEW

| Section | Topic | Time | Cumulative |
|---------|--------|------|------------|
| 1 | Context + Workflow | 6 min | 6 |
| 2 | Pain: What didn’t land + Reteach | 10 min | 16 |
| 3 | Recording + trust/privacy | 6 min | 22 |
| 4 | Validate outputs (ratings + tradeoffs) | 15 min | 37 |
| 5 | Integration constraints | 4 min | 41 |
| 6 | Competition/substitutes | 2 min | 43 |
| 7 | Pricing + wrap | 2 min | 45 |

**Stop condition:** At 37 minutes, skip to Section 6 (Competition) then 7 (Pricing). At 45 minutes, thank and end.

---

## SECTION 1 — Context + Workflow (6 min)

**Goal:** Understand their schedule, course load, and how they currently close the loop after a class (notes, grading, next-day prep).

1. “What do you teach, and what does a typical week look like in terms of number of classes and block vs period?”
2. “After a class ends, what do you actually do in the next 10–15 minutes — if anything — to capture what happened or what to do next?”
3. “How do you decide what to teach the next day — is it mostly pacing guide, or do you adjust based on how today went?”

**Follow-up only if needed:** “When you adjust based on how today went, where does that information live — in your head, notes, or somewhere else?”

**Stop:** At 6 min, say: “That’s really helpful. I want to dig into what happens when things don’t land.”

---

## SECTION 2 — Pain: What didn’t land + Reteach realities (10 min)

**Goal:** Understand how they detect confusion, how they reteach today, and the cost (time, precision) of current reteach.

4. “When a lesson doesn’t land well, how do you usually find out — during class, after, or when you grade?”
5. “When you do reteach, what does that look like — full re-cover of the concept, or something shorter and more targeted?”
6. “Roughly how much class time do you spend on reteach in a typical month, and how much of that do you wish was more targeted instead of re-covering everything?”
7. **Forced tradeoff:** “If you could have only one of these tomorrow: (a) a precise 10-minute reteach plan for one concept that didn’t land, or (b) a full set of exit ticket questions for that class — which would you use first and why?”

**Follow-up only if needed:** “What usually stops you from doing quick checks or exit tickets more often — time to make them, time to run them, or something else?”

**Stop:** At 16 min total, say: “I want to ask about how you’d feel about something that uses class audio or transcript to make those outputs.”

---

## SECTION 3 — Recording + trust/privacy (6 min)

**Goal:** Gauge comfort with class recording/transcript as input; surface surveillance and admin-sharing concerns.

8. “Would you be open to a tool that used a recording or transcript of your class to generate things like a reteach plan or exit ticket — or does that feel off from the start?”
9. “What would make you trust that this was for you only — e.g. no admin access, no sharing unless you click share?”
10. “Is there anything that would make you stop using it after one week — for example tone, feeling evaluated, or friction?”

**Stop:** At 22 min total, say: “I’m going to show you some example outputs and ask you to rate how useful each would be for you.”

---

## SECTION 4 — Validate outputs (15 min)

**Goal:** Get a 1–5 usefulness rating for every artifact; one forced tradeoff; identify “cool but ignored” and “would use every week.”

**Instructions to interviewer:** For each artifact, describe it in one sentence, then ask: “On a scale of 1–5, how useful would this be for you in your actual workflow — 1 = wouldn’t use, 5 = would use regularly?” Note the number. If they say “depends,” ask: “If you had it tomorrow for your last class, would you use it?” and then 1–5.

**Artifacts to rate (in this order):**

1. **Teacher summary + objectives** — “A short summary of what you taught plus the objectives from your lesson, with textbook refs.”
2. **Student recap** — “A ready-to-share recap for students: what we did, key vocab, what to study, 3 practice prompts, textbook page.”
3. **Time map** — “A breakdown of minutes per topic with off-track segments and optional ‘engagement’ framing.”
4. **Confusion moments + clarifications** — “Top 3 confusion moments with timestamps and short clarifying explanations.”
5. **Exit ticket (real-time)** — “5 questions you could show at end of class, aligned to what you actually taught.”
6. **Exit ticket (next-day warmup)** — “Same 5 questions + answer key to use as next-day warmup.”
7. **10-minute reteach plan** — “A targeted mini-lesson plan for one concept, with 2 options, based on confusion signals.”
8. **One coaching insight** — “One glow and one grow, evidence-based, with a timestamp — opt-in, not required.”
9. **Homework/quiz generator** — “AP/IB-style questions aligned to your objectives, with a note on student workload.”
10. **Talk ratio trend** — “A simple view of your talk vs student talk over the last few lessons.”
11. **PD/admin export** — “A report you own and choose to share — no admin access by default.”
12. **Assignment time estimator** — “You paste or upload an assignment; you get estimated completion time (e.g. p25/p50/p75) and levers to shorten or lengthen.”
13. **“Where did I leave off?” task state** — “A single list: what’s not graded, what reteach is planned, which objectives aren’t met — like an assistant, not a project manager.”
14. **Community / sharing layer** — “A place to share what worked with other teachers, on your terms — searchable, upvotes, no auto-sharing.”

**Forced tradeoffs (pick 2–3 max within the 15 min):**

- “If we could only build ONE of these first, which one would you want?”
- “Which two outputs would you use every week without fail?”
- “Which of these sounds ‘cool but I’d probably ignore it’?”
- “Which one would you pay for personally, if it was the only feature?”

**Stop:** At 37 min total, say: “Last two quick areas: how this would fit your tools, and what you pay for now.”

---

## SECTION 5 — Integration constraints (4 min)

**Goal:** Understand where they live (LMS, Drive, gradebook) and what “low friction” means for them.

11. “Where do you keep your lesson materials and objectives today — Google Drive, LMS, slides, somewhere else?”
12. “What would ‘low friction’ look like for you — e.g. start when you take attendance, or something else?”
13. “Any tools we’d need to work with or that you wouldn’t give up?”

**Stop:** At 41 min, say: “Quickly: what do you use instead of this today, and what would you pay?”

---

## SECTION 6 — Competition/substitutes (2 min)

**Goal:** What they use today (AI, exit tickets, PLC) and what would replace or complement ClassPal.

14. “What do you use today to get to ‘what do I do next’ or quick checks — AI tools, PLC, paper exit tickets, nothing?”
15. “What would have to be true for you to switch or add this on top of that?”

**Stop:** At 43 min, go to pricing.

---

## SECTION 7 — Pricing willingness + wrap (2 min)

**Goal:** Current out-of-pocket spend; “no second thought” price; “would stop” price; willingness to pay for time saved.

16. “Do you pay for any teaching or productivity tools out of pocket right now? If so, which and roughly how much?”
17. “At what monthly price would you not think twice about trying this? At what price would you definitely not pay?”
18. “If this saved you 20 minutes of reteach per week or prevented a half-class reteach once a month, what would you pay per month?”

**Wrap:** “Is there anything we didn’t ask that would matter for whether you’d use this?” Thank them and end by 45 min.

---

## REMINDERS FOR INTERVIEWER

- **No redundancy:** Don’t ask both “How do you detect confusion?” and “When a lesson goes badly how do you figure out why?” — use one and a short follow-up if needed.
- **Behavior over intent:** If they don’t use exit tickets, ask “What prevents you?” not “Would you use them?”
- **Tradeoffs:** Use the forced tradeoff questions in Section 4; they reveal priority better than “would you use this?”
- **Pricing:** Include all three pricing questions (current spend, no-second-thought price, pay for time saved).
- **Churn:** Section 3 question 10 captures “what would make you churn after week 1.”

End of Interview Script.
